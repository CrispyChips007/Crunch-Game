Welcome to Crunch

Pick scoring dice, then bank safely or risk another roll. Reach 10,000 in as few turns as you can—but a roll with no score is a Crunch.

How to play

  1. Roll all available dice.
  2. Select at least one scoring die.
  3. Risk it: roll the remaining dice to earn more points, or Bank to end your turn and add your unbanked points to your total.

  • To add points to your total for the first time, you must earn and Bank at least 1,000 points in one turn. After that, you may Bank any amount.
  • A roll with no scoring dice is a Crunch. You lose every unbanked point from that turn.
  • If all five dice score, you may roll again with all five dice.
